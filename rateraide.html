Rateraide

Rateraide is a time-tracking browser extension for search-quality raters. It runs alongside your work on RaterHub and keeps an accurate record of your sessions, tasks, and time — entirely on your own computer.

Rateraide is a Manifest V3 extension for Chromium-based browsers (Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge).

Features

  • Automatic session tracking: start/stop times, durations, and per-task-type counts as you acquire and submit tasks.
  • Earnings and AET (allotted estimated time) totals based on your configured rate.
  • A timesheet and summary dashboard for reviewing your history by day, week, and month.
  • A pop-out timer so you can keep an eye on the clock while you work.
  • Optional auto-stop — end the session when you Submit and Stop Rating, or when RaterHub runs out of tasks.
  • Optional, user-initiated Google Drive backup of your own data, so you never lose your history.

Privacy

All of your data is collected, stored, and processed locally on your device, and only locally. Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you choose to enable the optional Google Drive backup. See the full Rateraide Privacy Policy for details.
